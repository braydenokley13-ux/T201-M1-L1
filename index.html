🏀 CAP CRASH 🏀

Bow Sports Capital · Track 201 · Front Office Simulation

📰 Mission Briefing

Welcome to the front office. You've just been hired as a General Manager for a pro basketball team. Last season ended in a championship — but then the league's salary cap crashed to $120 million, and your team had to release every player to start fresh.

🎯 Your Job

Rebuild a smart, competitive roster under a tight budget. You'll sign, cut, and trade players, manage a real salary cap, and balance four things at once. There's no single perfect answer — your job is to pick a strategy and defend it.

📊 Watch Four Things

💰

Cap Space

Money left under the cap. Every dollar has a job.

🏆

Wins

How strong your team projects to be.

🤝

Chemistry

How well your players fit together.

🔥

Clout

How excited fans, media & sponsors are.

⚡ The Catch

No roster can max out all four. Spending big on stars wins games and clout — but drains cap space and can hurt chemistry. A cheaper, balanced team stays flexible — but may not win as much. Midway through, a trade deadline will test your plan with a real win-now vs. stay-flexible decision.

Build a strategy. Survive the pressure. Defend your plan.
